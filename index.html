About the festival

Celebrating Cameroonian Culture in the UK

CICFESTUK is the first Cameroon International Cultural Festival to be held in the United Kingdom. Bringing together music, dance, food, fashion and heritage in one incredible day of celebration in Milton Keynes.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can i be a vendor at the festival? ⌄

To become a vendor at the festival, please contact us via the contact section and provide details for the vendor application form about your business and the products or services you offer.

What should I wear? ⌄

Wear something comfortable, weather-appropriate, and feel free to celebrate in traditional Cameroonian attire if you'd like.

Can I volunteer? ⌄

Yes! We're always looking for volunteers to help. Get in touch with us via the contact section to register your interest.

Will there be food and drinks? ⌄

Yes! Our food village will offer a wide range of traditional Cameroonian delicacies and drinks throughout the day.

Do I need to buy tickets in advance? ⌄

We recommend booking in advance to guarantee entry, as capacity is limited. Tickets can be purchased through our Eventbrite link.

Who can attend? ⌄

Everyone is welcome, CICFEST UK is a family-friendly event open to Cameroonians and friends of Cameroon of all ages.
